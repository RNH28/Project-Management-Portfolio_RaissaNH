My project management approach

Systems for
successful delivery.

My project management approach is typically hybrid, combining structured project planning with Agile and iterative execution. I begin by defining project scope, deliverables, stakeholders, milestones, deadlines, and dependencies. Depending on project complexity, I use Microsoft Project, Asana, or custom Excel/Google Sheets trackers and SOPs to build schedules, establish dependencies, assign resources, track milestones, and monitor progress. For more complex schedules, I use Microsoft Project to evaluate critical paths, predecessor relationships, resource allocation, and performance against the original plan.:
